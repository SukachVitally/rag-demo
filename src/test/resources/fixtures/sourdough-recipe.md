# Weekend Sourdough Loaf

This is a simple recipe for a crusty sourdough loaf. It is used as unrelated test data so that retrieval has something to rank below the relevant document.

## Ingredients

- 500 g strong white bread flour
- 350 g water at room temperature
- 100 g active sourdough starter
- 10 g fine sea salt

## Method

Mix the flour and water and leave the dough to rest for one hour. Add the starter and salt, then fold the dough every thirty minutes for two hours. Shape the loaf, place it in a floured basket and prove it overnight in the fridge.

Bake in a preheated Dutch oven at 250 degrees for twenty minutes with the lid on, then for another twenty-five minutes with the lid off until the crust is deep brown. Let the bread cool for at least an hour before slicing.
